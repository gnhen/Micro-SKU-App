# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 23, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the unofficial Micro SKU Scanner / Micro-SKU-App. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or that you may provide when you use the app.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 No Automatic Collection
The app does not automatically collect, transmit, or process any personal information. All core functionality (barcode scanning, product lookups, lists, PC Builder, search history) operates entirely on your device.

### 2.2 Feedback Form (Optional)
When you voluntarily submit the in-app feedback form or send references, you may provide your name and selected store location. This data is sent directly to a third-party form handler (Formspree) and is subject to their privacy policy. You may leave the name field blank to remain anonymous.

### 2.3 Remote Availability Check
On launch, the app makes a lightweight HTTP request to the GitHub API to check whether the app is still available (the "kill switch" mechanism). This request does not include any personal data.

## 3. Data Usage
Any data processed by this application (such as SKU numbers or product searches) is used explicitly to query public or standard endpoints for product information. This query data is not associated with personal identities and is not retained on our servers.

## 4. Local Storage
The app utilizes local storage on your device to save:
- Department and tab customization preferences
- Store location selection
- Theme preference (including Windows XP mode)
- Search history (last 50 items)
- Item lists and PC builds
- Plans enabled/disabled state

All data remains on your device and is not transmitted externally.

## 5. Third-Party Services
The app connects to the following third-party services:
- **microcenter.com** — Product data, inventory, search results (via HTTP requests and WebView)
- **GitHub API** — Update availability check
- **Formspree** — Feedback form submission (when used)

Interactions with these services are subject to their respective privacy policies.

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our application is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to Our Privacy Policy
We may update the privacy policy from time to time. If we make material changes to how we treat user information, we will notify you by updating the effective date at the top of this policy.

## 8. Contact Information
If you have any questions or comments about this privacy policy and our privacy practices, please contact us via the in-app feedback form or the project repository.
